mushafi - مصحفي is an app dedicated to reading the Holy Quran and browsing its noble verses with ease, available via browser and as a standalone desktop application, with comprehensive support for dozens of languages and translations. We believe that user privacy is a fundamental right, and we are committed to full transparency regarding your data. This document explains precisely what we collect and what we don't, and how we handle any data.

1 Who We Are

mushafi - مصحفي is an app that allows users to read the Holy Quran, available in two versions: a web app used directly through the browser, and a standalone desktop app installed on the computer. The app is non-commercial, requires no registration or account creation, and is designed to be a clean, safe means of connecting with the Book of Allah.

2 What We Don't Collect — An Explicit Declaration

mushafi - مصحفي does not collect any personal data, including:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Passwords or account information — no login system exists
  • Payment data or financial information of any kind
  • Geographic location data
  • Browsing behavior or reading patterns
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising purposes
  • Any information that allows identification of your personal identity

3 Local Storage on Your Device

The app uses the local storage built into your browser or operating system to save your personal preferences between sessions. This data stays exclusively on your device and is never sent to any server at any time, including:

  • Anonymous user identifier for session tracking
  • Last surah or page you reached in your recitation
  • Selected language for the app interface
  • Preferred translation or commentary
  • Font size and display style preferences
  • Dark or light mode
  • App settings and reading preferences
  • Technical error logs for performance improvement

You can delete this data at any time from the app or browser settings without any external effect.

4 How We Use Information

The data saved locally on your device is used exclusively for:

  • Improving app performance and fixing bugs
  • Providing a personalized user experience
  • Sending new app updates

5 External Services

mushafi - مصحفي relies on external services to provide Quran texts, translations, and some technical resources. When loading this content, limited technical data may be recorded with these service providers according to their own privacy policies, which is outside the direct control of the app.

We strive to select highly reliable external services while minimizing dependence on them to the lowest possible level.

6 Cookies

mushafi - مصحفي does not use cookies for any tracking, analytical, or advertising purposes. The app is completely free from any analytics systems, advertising networks, or external tracking codes.

Some external services may independently create limited technical cookies, which is outside the app's direct control.

7 Data Security and Protection

mushafi - مصحفي applies technical security standards including:

  • Encrypted communications: All web version communications are encrypted with a valid SSL/TLS certificate
  • No user database: No system for registering accounts or storing user data exists
  • Regular updates: Continuous review of dependencies and libraries to ensure app security in both versions

All data is encrypted and protected. We do not sell your data to any third party and do not share it except when legally required.

8 Your Rights as a User

Since mushafi - مصحفي does not collect personal data, your privacy is fully protected from the outset. Regardless, you have the right to:

  • Inquiry: Ask at any time about the types of data we handle
  • Deletion: Remove local storage data from your device whenever you wish
  • Transparency: Access this policy and its updates at all times
  • Exit: Use or stop using the app without any restrictions or consequences

9 Children's Privacy

mushafi - مصحفي is suitable for all ages including children, as it is limited to presenting the Holy Quran. Since the app does not collect any personal data from any user regardless of age, it provides a naturally safe environment.

10 Updates and Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect any changes in the app or legal requirements. In the event of significant changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be updated
  • The new version will be published immediately
  • Your continued use of the app after the update is considered implicit consent to the updated policy
